MOSS-TTS-Nano host sampling and tokenization algorithm references

The Swift PCG64 sampler uses the PCG XSL-RR 128/64 algorithm and NumPy-compatible
SeedSequence mixing. The original implementations are available at:
https://github.com/numpy/numpy/tree/v1.26.4/numpy/random
https://www.pcg-random.org/
